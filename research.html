Overview

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence with research and development and with manufacturing. I am Digitalization Manager at BASF, a Visiting Scientist in Biological Sciences and an Adjunct Professor in Chemical Engineering at LSU.

AI meets manufacturing

With Professors Jose Romagnoli and Xun Tang in Chemical Engineering at LSU, we use machine learning to model chemical production. We predict quality and production variables that cannot be measured directly (soft sensors), detect anomalies in equipment sensor data, build dynamic dashboards for quality and production data, and use unsupervised clustering to approximate manufacturing operating conditions. We recently described a graph-temporal surrogate architecture for multivariate process modeling.

  • Pereira MVA, Dixon E, Wall PK, Tang X, Romagnoli JA. Topology aware graph-temporal surrogate architecture for multivariate process modeling. Computers & Chemical Engineering. 2026 Sep;216:109921. doi:10.1016/j.compchemeng.2026.109921

Computational biology

With Professors Alyssa Johnson and Adam Bohnert in Biological Sciences at LSU, we used RNA-seq in D. melanogaster and C. elegans to study differentially expressed genes under different biological conditions. We built pipelines for enriched gene sets (KEGG, GO) and for co-expressed gene networks, and we looked for conserved non-coding regulatory elements in those networks. This work contributed to two 2025 studies: lysosome regulation in C. elegans and a short-sleep mutation in Drosophila.

Earlier work

As lead bioinformatician on NSF-funded Plant Genome Projects at Penn State (2001-2009), I developed bioinformatics pipelines and visualization tools for genome annotation and analysis, and I helped annotate and publish the poplar and papaya genomes. I built the PlantTribes gene-family database, which we used to publish gene-family papers on the MADS-box and APETALA2-like transcription factors, and I worked on early genome duplications and early diversification in flowering plants. I also trained and supervised dozens of students in bioinformatics, database management and web development. See Publications for the full list.

About

I am a Bioinformatician, Information Architect, Data Scientist and Software Engineer with 25+ years of experience at two major universities in genomics and bioinformatics (Penn State and LSU), at BASF, and at a software startup (Vedalabs). My background combines biology, engineering and computer science, and I have used all three throughout my career.

I architect and build bioinformatics and industrial data solutions, from requirements through deployment. I have worked with relational databases and several programming languages, and I choose open-source software when it fits. I mentor development teams on best practice and lead cross-functional teams.

My work has three parts. At BASF, I lead digitalization, including industrial AI and knowledge management. In Chemical Engineering at LSU, we apply machine learning to process data (soft sensors, anomaly detection and surrogate models) with Professors Jose Romagnoli and Xun Tang. In Biological Sciences at LSU, we studied gene regulation in C. elegans and D. melanogaster with Professors Alyssa Johnson and Adam Bohnert, which contributed to two 2025 papers.
